# Privacy Policy — BubbleGames

**Last updated:** August 18, 2026

RealLifeCorp ("we", "us", "our") develops and publishes the mobile game **BubbleGames** (the "App"). This Privacy Policy explains what information the App handles, how it is used, and the choices available to you. We have designed BubbleGames to be privacy-respecting: the App has no advertising, no analytics, no user accounts, and does not send your personal data to our servers.

If you have any questions about this policy, contact us at **reallifecorp@gmail.com**.

---

## Summary

- We do **not** show advertising.
- We do **not** use analytics or tracking SDKs.
- We do **not** operate user accounts or a personal-data backend.
- The only information that leaves your device is your chosen username, and it is transmitted **only over your local network** to other players in the same local multiplayer session. It never reaches the internet or our servers.
- Purchases are processed by the app store (Google Play / Apple) and managed through RevenueCat. We do not collect or store your payment card details.
- For players identified as children, the username is automatically generated and cannot be changed, so no personal information can be entered.

---

## Information We Handle

### Stored locally on your device

The following is stored on your device (using local storage / shared preferences) and is **not** transmitted to us:

- **Username** — either chosen by you or automatically generated by the App (see "Children" below).
- **Screen size** — a technical value used to display the game correctly on your device.

This information stays on your device and is removed if you uninstall the App.

### Transmitted over the local network

- **Username** — When you play a local multiplayer session, your username is shared over your **local network (Wi-Fi / LAN)** with the other players connected to that same session, so it can be displayed in-game. This communication is peer-to-peer on the local network only. It does **not** pass through the internet, and it does **not** reach any server operated by us or any third party.

### Purchase information

BubbleGames offers a one-time in-app purchase that unlocks premium content. Purchases are handled as follows:

- Payments are processed by the **app store** (Google Play or the Apple App Store). We never receive or store your payment card or financial details.
- We use **RevenueCat** to manage and validate purchases and to determine whether you have unlocked premium content. RevenueCat assigns an **anonymous, randomly generated identifier** (a UUID) to your installation. This identifier does not contain your name, email, or any personal information, and no advertising identifier is used.
- We collect the **purchase identifier** associated with your transaction for the sole purpose of unlocking the premium content you have paid for.

RevenueCat acts as a third-party processor for this purpose. You can review RevenueCat's privacy practices at https://www.revenuecat.com/privacy.

Because the App has no user accounts, purchases are tied to your app store account. If you reinstall the App or switch devices, use the **Restore Purchases** option to recover your premium access.

## How We Use Information

We use the information described above only to:

- Run the game and display it correctly on your device.
- Show your username to other players during a local multiplayer session.
- Unlock and restore the premium content you have purchased.

We do **not** use your information for advertising, profiling, analytics, or any form of tracking.

---

## Sharing of Information

We do not sell your information and we do not share it for advertising. Information is only shared in these limited ways:

- **With other local players:** your username, over the local network, during a multiplayer session.
- **With the app store and RevenueCat:** purchase-related information, solely to process and validate your purchase.

---

## Children's Privacy

BubbleGames is family-friendly and intended for a general audience, which may include children.

When you first open the App, we ask for your age in a neutral way. If the App determines that the user is under the applicable age threshold:

- The username is **automatically generated by the App and cannot be changed**, so a child cannot enter their real name or any other personal information.
- As a result, **no personal information from a child is entered, stored, or transmitted** over the local network.

We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. Because children cannot enter a custom username, and because the App has no accounts, no analytics, and no advertising, the App does not collect personal information that would identify a child.

In-app purchases in the children's experience are subject to the parental authentication controls provided by the app store.

If you believe a child has provided personal information in a way not described here, please contact us at **reallifecorp@gmail.com** and we will address it.

---

## Data Retention

Locally stored information (username, screen size) remains on your device until you delete it or uninstall the App. Purchase records are retained by the app store and RevenueCat according to their own policies.

- You can manage or request deletion of purchase-related data by contacting the app store or RevenueCat, or by writing to us at the address below.
